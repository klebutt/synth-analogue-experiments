How validators score

Validators use CRPS on price change in basis points over four intervals: 5m, 30m, 3h, 24h. Prompt score = sum of CRPS over these intervals. Dashboard uses the same definition (approximated from mean/p10/p90). Lower = better.

Actuals from Yahoo Finance; validators use Pyth. BTC/ETH/SOL are comparable; XAU and tokenised equities may differ.
